### Nota · pagina 8

> Bloom filters

**Explanation**

### Bloom Filters in GPT-2 Memorization Analysis
**Purpose**:
- Detect overlap between test datasets and WebText training data.
- Quantify potential memorization (vs. generalization) in GPT-2.

**Method**:
- Stored 8-grams (8-token sequences) from WebText training in Bloom filters.
- False-positive rate capped at \(10^{-8}\) for reliability.
- Measured % of test-set 8-grams appearing in training.

**Key Findings**:
- Common NLP test sets (e.g., PTB, WikiText-2) had **1–6% overlap** with WebText.
- Overlap was comparable to standard train-test splits (e.g., WikiText-103: 9.09% internal overlap).
- Minimal impact on GPT-2’s zero-shot performance (e.g., LAMBADA accuracy dropped only 0.3% after removing overlapping examples).

**Connection to Core Argument**:
Supports the claim that GPT-2’s success stems from **generalization** (learning tasks from diverse data) rather than data memorization. Low contamination validates its zero-shot capabilities.

---
**Note**: Bloom filters enabled efficient large-scale duplication analysis without exhaustive searches.
